Relationships Reveal Our Old Wounds

Why do we keep putting the people who love us through tests — and where does that fear of rejection come from?

Dr. Demy Adel · 3 November 2026 · 3 min read

Relationships are the space that exposes every trauma that has shaped us — the kind you might think about and say, “No, I don’t think what happened to me is the reason.” It may well turn out to be.

The test that never ends

Constantly putting others through tests of their patience and their love is nothing but a fear of rejection and a fear of an old pain repeating. That pain came from unstable treatment by a father or a mother:

  • The hours when dad was home were tense, and your nervous system was on alert.

  • Treatment that swung with his mood that day.

  • Threats of abandonment, emotional manipulation, blame, intimidation through religion and punishment.

These and other parental behaviours left us afraid of rejection and of being hurt again. So when you finally find a safe space — or simply a different one — you keep testing how long it will hold.

This is what normal looks like. You just need to understand that it was the old that wasn’t normal.

Instead of reinforcing it with behaviours in your present, go back and work on the old. It’s time to experience healthy relationships.
