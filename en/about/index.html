About Demy

Demy Adel is a trauma therapist and a member of the Egyptian Society of Psychotherapists. She writes and speaks about relationships, attachment, old wounds and self-worth — in plain language that stays close to everyday life.

On Instagram she shares short reflections almost every day, and on Al Malak Youth Radio she hosts conversations about feelings and relationships. All sessions are online, so you can book from anywhere. [Demy: add your story, training and experience in your own words]
